Privacy Notice (Draft)

Prototype notice · review before public release

This draft describes the current prototype. It is not a final privacy policy or legal advice.

Information used

The portal reads coach and device configuration, including coach identifiers and specifications, device types, network addresses, indexes, and device names. It reads coach.json and devices.json from the connected bridge and refreshes them periodically.

How information is used

The portal displays the configuration in your browser. When you rename a device, its new name and its type, source address, and RVC index are saved in devices.json. Coach details, tank capacities, cover timings, and battery specifications are saved in coach.json.

Storage and retention

Configuration is stored on the bridge filesystem. The page holds fetched data in browser memory while it is open. This prototype does not set cookies, create user accounts, or include analytics code. Bridge access logs and browser-managed storage are outside this page's control.

Third-party services

The page currently requests fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google's font services to retrieve those assets; Google may receive connection information such as your IP address. Consider self-hosting the fonts or removing the external request before release.

Operator and contact

The responsible operator, privacy contact, applicable regions, and any retention or deletion contact are not yet specified. Add these details and have the final notice reviewed for the places where the portal will be offered.
